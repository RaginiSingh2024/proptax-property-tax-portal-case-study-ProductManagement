Help & Support

Go to Search Property and enter the property ID, owner name, ward, or address to view matching records.

Open the Self-Assessment section, fill in the required property details, validate the form, and continue to tax calculation.

Use the Tax Calculator and enter property type, built-up area, age, and usage type. The system shows a sample prototype calculation.

Select your payment method in the payment portal, confirm the amount, and complete the demo payment flow.

After successful payment, click View Receipt or access the Receipts section to print or download the digital receipt.

Use the Objections page to submit a claim, attach support details, and track the status until municipal review is completed.
